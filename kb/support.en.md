## kb:account_deletion
title: Account deletion
source: Support operations guide
reviewed: 2026-10-01
validUntil: 2027-06-30

You can delete your account yourself after signing in, under [Settings > Account > Delete account]. Deleting your account removes your data and subscription information, and deleted data cannot be recovered. If you have an active paid subscription, please cancel it first so the deletion can complete. For identity verification, deletion only works while you are signed in with the email address you registered.

## kb:receipt
title: Receipts and tax invoices
source: Billing FAQ
reviewed: 2026-10-01
validUntil: 2027-06-30

You can download a PDF receipt for each payment under [Settings > Billing > Payment history]. If you need a tax invoice, use [Request tax invoice] on the same screen and enter your business details; the invoice is sent to your registered email once it is issued. All past payments are listed on the same screen.

## kb:cancel_subscription
title: Cancelling a subscription
source: Billing FAQ
reviewed: 2026-10-01
validUntil: 2027-06-30

To cancel, go to [Settings > Billing > Subscription] and select [Cancel subscription]. You can keep using the service until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged from the next billing date. Your data is kept after cancellation, and you can subscribe again at any time.

## kb:login
title: Sign-in problems
source: Support operations guide
reviewed: 2026-10-01
validUntil: 2027-06-30

If you cannot sign in, first check that you are using the email address you registered with. If you forgot your password, use [Reset password] on the sign-in screen to receive a reset email. If the email does not arrive, please check your spam and promotions folders; if it is still missing, your company mail server may be blocking it, so try another address or let us know.

## kb:features
title: Using features
source: Help center
reviewed: 2026-10-01
validUntil: 2027-06-30

The main features are available from the top menu of the dashboard, and [Help] at the top right of each screen explains that screen. Step-by-step guides are collected in the help center (https://help.example.com/guide). If you cannot find a feature, tell us what you want to do and we will point you to the right one.
